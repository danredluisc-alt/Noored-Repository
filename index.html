Malunggay Pandesal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup malunggay leaves, chopped

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, combine warm milk, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and salt. Add the yeast mixture and melted butter. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Punch down the dough and divide it into small portions. Roll each portion into a ball and flatten slightly.
  6. Place the flattened dough balls on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Sprinkle chopped malunggay leaves on top of each ball.
  7. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown. Remove from the oven and let them cool slightly before serving.
